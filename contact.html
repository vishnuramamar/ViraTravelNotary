Frequently Asked Questions

Can I come to you instead?

Absolutely! You can visit us at our location (no travel fee) or we can come to you for added convenience (travel fee applies).

What are your fees?

Pricing includes standard notary fees plus travel costs for mobile visits. Contact us at (951) 523-9466 for a quote based on your specific needs and location.

Do you charge a travel fee?

Yes, travel fees apply when we come to your location. No travel fee if you visit us! We'll provide a complete quote upfront with no hidden charges.

How quickly can you help me?

We strive to accommodate same-day or next-day appointments whenever possible. Contact us to check availability.

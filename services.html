Acknowledgments

An acknowledgment verifies that you willingly signed a document and that your identity has been confirmed.

  • Identity verification
  • Signature witnessing
  • Official notary seal
  • Proper documentation
  • Legal compliance
  • Professional certification
📝

Jurats (Sworn Statements)

A jurat certifies that you signed a document in the notary's presence and swore or affirmed that the contents are true.

  • Oath administration
  • Affirmation services
  • Affidavit notarization
  • Sworn statements
  • Legal declarations
  • Court documents
📄

Copy Certification

Certification that a copy of an original document is a true and accurate reproduction (as permitted by California law).

  • Certified copies of documents
  • Power of attorney copies
  • Notarial records
  • As allowed by state law
